Uganda's 2026 tax changes: what we know so far

Several tax amendments apply from 1 July 2026. The points below come from the Bills published by Parliament (Bills Supplement No. 2, 27 March 2026) and from professional reports of the Acts as passed. The gazetted Acts were not yet available to us, so check figures against them before relying on them.

  • PAYE: the tax-free part of monthly pay rises from UGX 235,000 to UGX 335,000, with new bands above it.
  • VAT registration threshold: raised from UGX 150 million a year. The published Bill said UGX 250 million; reports of the passed Act say UGX 300 million. Confirm which before deciding whether to register or deregister.
  • New withholding taxes on betting winnings, telecom and mobile-money commissions and payments to public entertainers.
  • Excise duty rises on fuel, cement, sugar, cooking oil and other goods.

TaxComp shows each change with its source and status, and works out how it affects you: Tax Impact Assessment (free account). Or talk to us.

    Privacy policy

    • Calculator figures are worked out in your browser and are not uploaded.
    • The audit and tax-health checklist saves your answers in this browser only.
    • Your account (email and subscription) is held in our Supabase database; payments are processed by Flutterwave, and TaxComp does not see card or Mobile Money PINs.
    • AI questions are sent through Cloudflare to Anthropic Claude (Google Gemini as backup). Live web research runs only when you opt in. Your conversation is kept in this browser only, separately for each account, so it is there when you come back; Clear removes it, and signing out deletes it from the device. It is not stored on TaxComp's servers.
    • If you agree, a question TaxComp could not answer is saved for research, visible only to administrators, and deleted after 90 days.
    • Firm workspace records are stored in the firm's private workspace and are visible only to that firm's members.
    • Challenge an Assessment: you agree before your first upload. Notices, records and your answers are kept in TaxComp's private Supabase database and private file storage (EU, Ireland) until you delete the case; one button deletes the case and all its files. Only you and, if your case is assigned to one, an Opitekene reviewer can see them, and every reviewer view, change and download is logged. When you ask us to read a notice, it is sent to Anthropic's Claude (and to Google Gemini's paid service only if Opitekene has turned it on), neither of which uses it to train its models. Uploaded documents and case data are never stored in the app's offline cache.
