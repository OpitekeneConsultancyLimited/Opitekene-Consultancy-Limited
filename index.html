WHAT CHANGED

FY 2026/27 updates

These amendments generally took effect on 1 July 2026. This site applies them to estimates where relevant.

01

VAT registration thresholds increased

UGX 300 million in annual taxable supplies or more than UGX 75 million in a three-month period.

02

VAT withholding narrowed

VAT withholding applies to relevant taxable-supply payments where the supplier has not issued an EFRIS e-invoice or e-receipt. Input-credit recovery is also affected by e-invoice evidence and exempt supplies.

03

New withholding categories

Includes resident public entertainers and betting or gaming winnings, plus special rules for certain non-resident debenture interest and insurance-agent commissions.

04

Individual rental tax filings are monthly

Monthly filing and payment apply from 1 July 2026. The rental calculator still shows an annual estimate; confirm monthly obligations with URA.

05

Targeted VAT exemptions

The 2026 amendments add VAT-exempt supplies for specified nuclear-energy contractors and subcontractors. Confirm that the transaction qualifies before classifying it as exempt.
