# Tax rules verification register

The executable tax rules currently contain 43 FY 2026/27 records. Every record remains `pending-review`; no record is approved for filing. The configured dates are not independently verified statutory effective dates.

The rule-by-rule verification matrix, required evidence fields, source/provision gaps, amendment status, computation impact and reviewer requirements are maintained in [`LEGAL_REVIEW_REGISTER.md`](./LEGAL_REVIEW_REGISTER.md).

Do not treat AI interpretation, secondary-source citations, passing tests or this inventory as legal verification. A qualified Ugandan tax/legal reviewer must verify each rule against an official source and record the provision, effective period, amendments, methodology, reviewer, review date and rationale before changing its status.
