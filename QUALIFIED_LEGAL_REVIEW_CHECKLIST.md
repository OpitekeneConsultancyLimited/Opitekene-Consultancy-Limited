# Qualified Uganda tax/legal review checklist

Date: 2026-11-07  
Status: **AWAITING QUALIFIED EXTERNAL REVIEW**

Use one signed review record per rule in `TAX_RULE_VERIFICATION_REGISTER.xlsx`. Do not approve a batch based only on title or tax head.

| Review evidence | Required |
|---|---|
| Reviewer qualification, identity and independence | Record name/role/qualification and review date. |
| Official source | Attach/identify official Gazette, legislation, URA publication, treaty, regulation or court judgment; include stable URL/document hash and issuing authority. |
| Exact provision | Record section/subsection/schedule or judgment paragraph and explain why it applies. |
| Effective period | Verify publication, commencement, effective-from/to dates and the taxpayer/transaction/date scope. |
| Amendment history | Identify each relevant amending instrument, commencement, repeal/supersession and transitional rule. |
| Computation impact | Independently recompute the formula, rate/base/threshold/relief conditions and edge cases; note rounding and assumptions. |
| Tax treatment | Distinguish statutory rule from URA administrative guidance, case interpretation, assumptions and AI-generated summary. |
| Historical coverage | Verify all periods used by the calculator; record gaps or unresolved conflicts. |
| Approval outcome | Mark verified only if evidence is complete; otherwise pending, rejected or superseded with reason. |
| Audit trail | Record approver, date, rationale, evidence version/hash and second reviewer if required by policy. |

No reviewer or review date has been recorded for any of the 43 current rule records. Never use an AI response, secondary summary, user-supplied reviewer name, or passing software test as legal approval.
