# Uganda legal-source gap report

Date: 2026-11-07  
Status: **BLOCKED — no filing-approved rule corpus**

## Inventory

`tax-rules.js` currently returns 43 FY 2026/27 rule records. All remain pending review and have no verified effective dates. The detailed row-by-row register is `LEGAL_REVIEW_REGISTER.md`; its spreadsheet companion is `TAX_RULE_VERIFICATION_REGISTER.xlsx`.

For every executable rule, the verified official source, exact section/provision, official URL/document, commencement/effective date, amendment chain, tax-year applicability, qualified reviewer, review date and approval rationale are missing or unestablished. Some rules cite secondary summaries; those are not primary legal verification.

## Scope gaps

- Income Tax Act, VAT Act and Tax Procedures Code Act: no validated provision-to-rule mapping.
- Stamp Duty, Excise Duty, EAC customs, regulations and Finance/amendment Acts: no complete source/effective-period corpus.
- URA publications, EFRIS guidance and administrative notices: no verified ingestion; must remain distinguished from legislation.
- DTA, transfer pricing and cross-border rules: no verified treaty/source mapping or historical applicability matrix.
- Industrial building deductions and capital gains: executable provisional inputs exist, but statutory treatment and effective periods are unapproved.
- Judicial decisions: no complete independently reviewed judgment corpus; summaries must not be presented as holdings without primary judgment review.
- Historical rules: no complete amendment/commencement history across relevant tax years.

## Closure

Acquire authoritative Gazette, Uganda legislation, URA and court records; preserve source documents and hashes; map each rule to the exact provision and effective periods; document amendments and computation effects; obtain qualified Ugandan tax/legal review per record; independently reconcile historical periods. Keep all rule records pending until approval.
